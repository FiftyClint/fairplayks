Private School Championship Dominance in Kansas (3A–5A)

Statistical analysis of KSHSAA championship results supporting HB 2176 / SB 349. Data covers 2001–2025 across 136 schools in classes 3A, 4A, and 5A — the only classes impacted by the proposed legislation.

⚖️
The Baseline: Private schools are 11 of 136 schools in 3A–5A — just 8.1%. If competition were fair, they'd win roughly 8% of championships. Instead, they win 34.7% — a 4.3× overrepresentation. This page explains why.

The Structural Advantage: Talent Pool Disparity
Why private schools dominate — they recruit from entire metro areas while public schools serve defined districts
Average Talent Pool per School — Private vs. Public
KSHSAA classifies schools by enrollment, but private schools recruit from populations 28× to 99× larger than the public schools they compete against. A private school with 250 students has the same enrollment as a rural public school — but draws from a metro area of 500,000+.
